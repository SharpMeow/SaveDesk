Get your likes from X

Open X’s step-by-step instructions ↗

Choose your file

Already have it? Select your X archive ZIP or the like.js file inside its data folder. We read the likes files and skip the other archive entries.

A SaveDesk backup works too. ZIPs up to 200 MB; JSON and JS files up to 50 MB. Your archive is read on this device. If you sign in, imported saves sync to your account.

Make yourself at home

A small guide to SaveDesk.

  1. Add your saves. Choose an X archive, an export, or a SaveDesk backup. No GitHub account is needed.
  2. Find an idea. Search words, use @ for an author or # for a topic, or choose a filter. Press ⌘/Ctrl+K to jump to search.
  3. Make it yours. Add topics and mark posts as read. The Unread tab keeps your next reads together.
  4. Take a few ideas elsewhere. Select cards and choose “Copy for an AI chat.” Paste the text into any chat you choose. Nothing is sent automatically.
  5. Keep or share a copy. Download a backup for yourself, or a shareable page for someone else. They can open that page in a browser.

Without an account, your library lives in this browser. Sign-in on a configured site syncs your account library across devices. Keep a backup for an extra copy.
